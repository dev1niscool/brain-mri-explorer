BRAIN2PRINT STL PRINT NOTES

These two files come from the same saved Brain2Print reconstruction of the
public T1 MRI. They are experimental models; upper cortical tissue is missing.
Neither option reconstructs that missing anatomy.

CHOOSE A FILE
- b2p-print-full.stl: 96,828 triangles; 4,841,484 bytes.
  Exact triangle coordinates from the saved Brain2Print MZ3. Already below 100k
  triangles, so this is the default when your slicer handles it comfortably.
- b2p-print-compact.stl: 40,000 triangles; 2,000,084 bytes.
  About 59% fewer triangles. Topology-preserving quadric reduction, with no
  smoothing, hole filling or rescaling. Compared with Full, sampled nearest-
  surface deviation is 0.146 mm at the 95th percentile and 1.082 mm maximum.
  These are geometric comparisons, not anatomical accuracy estimates or a
  guaranteed continuous surface-distance bound.

IMPORT AND CHECK SCALE
1. Import either STL into your slicer and interpret coordinates as millimeters.
   STL does not store a unit declaration.
2. Before rotating the full model, its XYZ size is
   141.031 x 161.698 x 121.939 mm
   (right-left x front-back x bottom-top). The compact edition is
   141.013 x 161.647 x 122.023 mm.
3. Keep uniform scaling enabled. At 50%, all dimensions halve; the full edition
   becomes 70.52 x 80.85 x 60.97 mm.
   Smaller scale also makes grooves and thin features harder to print.
4. The anatomical coordinate frame is retained, so the mesh is not centered on
   the printer bed. Use your slicer's center-on-bed and drop-to-bed commands.

SLICE BEFORE PRINTING
5. Select your printer, nozzle and material profile. Choose orientation and
   supports in the slicer; the underside is curved and this is not a flat-base
   or support-free design. Review contact points, unsupported islands and thin
   features in the layer preview before printing. Verify that every intended
   part is present in the sliced result.
6. Both exports passed closed-surface, winding, connected-component and Euler
   topology checks. Those checks are not a printer-specific guarantee. If the
   slicer reports a repair or discards features, inspect the changed preview;
   do not assume that automatic repair preserves the original surface.
7. Use Compact to reduce polygon-processing cost. Reducing triangle count is
   not the same as lowering layer height, adding MRI detail or reducing plastic
   use at a fixed model size and print profile.

FITTED DISPLAY STAND
Use b2p-display-stand.stl with b2p-print-full.stl. The b2p-display-kit.zip download
contains this exact Full brain, its fitted three-point stand, and these notes.
Print brain and stand at the same uniform percentage. Put the stand's flat
base on the bed; use center-on-bed and drop-to-bed without rotating the stand.
After printing, rest the brain gently on the three fitted caps. It lifts off
vertically; no holes, sockets or glue are required. Inspect the fit gently and
adjust printing high spots or add thin felt if needed. Fit and stability were
checked geometrically; the combination has not been physically print-tested.
See b2p-display-stand.json for the exact source hash, clearance and fit checks.

The Compact edition changes local surfaces and is not the fitted reference.
The older site's stand and split halves were fitted to a different generated
brain mesh; they are separate accessories and are not this Brain2Print kit.
